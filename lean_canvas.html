📄 Concept Note — Smart Urban Traffic Forecasting System

Project Title: Smart Urban Traffic Congestion & Resource Demand Forecasting System

SDG Alignment: Goal 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities (Target 11.2 — Safe & Sustainable Transport)

Problem: Rapid urbanization has led to severe traffic congestion in Indian and global cities. Municipal authorities currently operate reactively — they respond to gridlocks only after they occur. There is no proactive, data-driven system to predict and prevent congestion before it happens. This results in wasted fuel, increased CO₂ emissions, delayed emergency vehicles, and poor quality of urban life.

Solution: This project builds a Machine Learning-powered Traffic Forecasting System that analyzes historical traffic patterns, weather data, and time-based features to predict vehicle volume 2–4 hours in advance. The system classifies congestion severity as Low, Medium, or High and provides automated re-routing recommendations through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

Technology: Python with XGBoost and Random Forest achieving R²=0.9670, engineered features including lag values, rolling statistics, and cyclical time encoding, visualized through Folium heatmap and Streamlit dashboard. LSTM neural network architecture also designed for deep sequential learning.

Impact: Municipal traffic authorities receive actionable alerts before congestion peaks — enabling proactive signal timing, route diversions, and resource pre-positioning. Directly reduces vehicular idling, lowers greenhouse gas emissions, and supports SDG 11.2.
